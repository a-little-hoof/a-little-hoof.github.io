Research

Generative models that are efficient, controllable, and grounded in theory.

My work spans representation learning in diffusion transformers, one-step and few-step generation, and distribution coverage in image and video models. For video generation, I study long-horizon memory and the computational cost of learning hidden-state reasoning.
